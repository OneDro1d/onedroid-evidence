Limits

Same sources only: the reviewers read what the company published and nothing else. They did not test the product, interview anyone or use outside records. Every verdict is about the sources as fetched on 2026-10-27; a page can change after that. One run is a sample of a moving site: a claim not listed may be right or wrong. The reviewers are AI agents run by OneDroid, checked by hand on a sample; OneDroid is not independent of its own method.
